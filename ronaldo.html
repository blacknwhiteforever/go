About

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro (born 5 February 1985) is widely regarded as one of the greatest football players of all time. The 41-year-old Portuguese forward captains both the Saudi Pro League club Al-Nassr and the Portugal national team. Often designated by his moniker CR7, Ronaldo is the highest goalscorer in the history of professional football, standing on the verge of the historic milestone of 1,000 career goals.

Current Career & Statistics

Ronaldo continues to play at a prolific rate. As of late 2026, his updated professional statistics highlight his ongoing role for club and country:

  • Total Career Goals: 985 official goals in 1,343 senior appearances.
  • Current Club Form (2026–27): Leading the line for Al-Nassr with 8 goals in 9 Saudi Pro League matches, recently securing a hat-trick in a 5–3 win against Al-Kholood.
  • International Record: All-time top scorer in men's international football history with 146 goals for Portugal, having recently led his country through the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Personal Background & Legacy

Ronaldo was born in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal into a family that faced deep financial hardships. He overcame severe adversity in childhood—including being diagnosed with a racing heart condition at age 15 that required surgery—to debut professionally for Sporting Lisbon in 2002. Known for his legendary training ethic, elite athleticism, and strict physical discipline, he has translated his sporting success into unparalleled global fame, becoming the most followed individual on social media worldwide.
