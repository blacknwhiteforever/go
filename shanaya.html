About

Shanaya Kapoor (born 3 November 1999) is an Indian actress, model, and entrepreneur who belongs to the illustrious Kapoor film dynasty. The 26-year-old actress is the daughter of Bollywood actor-producer Sanjay Kapoor and jewelry designer Maheep Kapoor. Initially gaining massive public visibility as a Gen-Z fashion icon and social media personality, she has established her presence in Hindi cinema through intense survival thrillers and romantic dramas.

🎬 Film & Web Career

Before stepping in front of the camera, Shanaya began her journey in the film industry behind the scenes, working as an assistant director on the 2020 film Gunjan Saxena: The Kargil Girl, which starred her cousin Janhvi Kapoor.

  • Theatrical Debut (2025): After initially announced projects like Bedhadak were shelved in pre-production, she made her official acting debut starring as Saba Shergill in Aankhon Ki Gustaakhiyan. Acting opposite Vikrant Massey, she portrayed a theater artist in the romance drama adapted from a Ruskin Bond short story.
  • Streaming Success (2026): In February 2026, she found critical and commercial success on Netflix with Bejoy Nambiar's survival thriller Tu Yaa Main. Co-starring Adarsh Gourav, the film reached the number one trending spot on the platform.
  • Upcoming Projects: Shanaya is set to make a highly anticipated web series debut in Karan Johar’s dark reimagining, Student of the Year: After Dark. Her upcoming pipeline also includes a high-octane zombie comedy alongside Tiger Shroff and the period comedy JC.
💼 Entrepreneurship & Fashion

Beyond acting, Shanaya has built a prominent professional footprint across the corporate and luxury lifestyle spaces:

  • Indinoor Jewellery: In 2025, she officially transitioned into business by co-founding the jewelry brand Indinoor, actively designing its 925 silver and festive bridal collections.
  • Brand Endorsements: She serves as the prominent brand ambassador for major global and Indian labels, including Puma, Samsung, Redken India, and Steve Madden.
  • Accolades: Her prominent style quotient earned her the Rising Star honor at the ELLE Beauty Awards and the Fresh Face title at the Femina Beauty Awards.
👥 Family Connections

Shanaya is surrounded by prominent Bollywood figures:

  • Father: Sanjay Kapoor, a well-known Indian actor and film producer who has worked extensively in Hindi cinema and television since the 1990s.
  • Paternal Uncles: Anil Kapoor (globally recognized superstar actor) and Boney Kapoor (prominent Bollywood film producer).
  • Uncle by Marriage: Sandeep Marwah (married to aunt Reena Kapoor), a media businessman and founder of Noida Film City.
  • Cousins: Bollywood actors Sonam Kapoor, Janhvi Kapoor, Khushi Kapoor, and Arjun Kapoor.
  • Inner Circle: She is famously childhood best friends with fellow new-generation actresses Ananya Panday and Suhana Khan.
