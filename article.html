How to Take a Screenshot on Windows, Mac & Mobile

Taking a screenshot is one of the most common tasks we do on our devices every day. Whether you want to save a funny conversation, capture an error message, or share a moment with friends, knowing the right shortcuts is essential.

1. How to Screenshot on Windows

If you are using Windows 10 or Windows 11, you have multiple options:

  • Print Screen (PrtScn): Press this key to copy the entire screen to your clipboard. Then paste it into Paint or Word.
  • Windows + Shift + S: This opens the Snipping Tool, allowing you to select a specific area of the screen.

2. How to Screenshot on Mac

On macOS, Apple makes it very simple:

  • Command + Shift + 3: Captures the entire screen and saves it to your desktop.
  • Command + Shift + 4: Allows you to select a specific area.

3. How to Screenshot on Mobile (iOS & Android)

For iPhone users, press the Side Button and Volume Up button simultaneously. For most Android devices, press the Power and Volume Down buttons at the same time.

Now that you know the basics, you can easily capture anything on your screen without hassle!
